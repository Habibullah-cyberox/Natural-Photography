THE PHOTOGRAPHER

Photographing places where light meets silence.

NatureLens is a digital photography journal dedicated to landscapes, forests, oceans, wildlife, deserts and frozen frontiers.

Every book combines photographs with the stories, locations, camera settings and experiences behind the images. Our mission is to inspire conservation through visual storytelling.

Founded in 2014, NatureLens has grown from a personal blog into a collective of five professional photographers across four continents. We believe that understanding a place is the first step toward protecting it.
